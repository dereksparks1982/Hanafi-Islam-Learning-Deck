# 99 Names of Allah Expansion — Source Notes

## Scope
Cards 1–12 only. This expansion is separate from the Main Deck and all other expansions.

## Source rule used for this expansion
- Qur'an 7:180: Allah has the Most Beautiful Names and believers are instructed to call upon Him by them.
- Sahih al-Bukhari 2736 and Sahih Muslim 2677: authentic hadith establishing ninety-nine Names and the virtue of enumerating them.
- Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3507: used for the traditional 99-name sequence in Cards 2–12.

## Important hadith note
The basic hadith concerning ninety-nine Names is authentic. The explicit enumeration attached in Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3507 is not at the same level of authenticity; it is graded weak in the Darussalam grading displayed by Sunnah.com, and al-Tirmidhi himself comments on the chain and other versions.

For that reason:
- this expansion labels the list as the traditional enumeration;
- it does not claim this is the only valid list;
- it does not claim Allah's Names are limited to ninety-nine.

## Translation note
English meanings are deliberately short study glosses. Divine Names can carry richer meanings than a one-line English translation can express.

## Numbering
1 — Expansion Guide
2–12 — eleven cards, nine Names each, total 99 Names
