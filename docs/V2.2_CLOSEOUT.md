# Hanafi Learning Deck v2.2 Closeout

## Status

**v2.2 is closed and accepted.**

Closeout authorized by the maintainer on **2026-09-25**. v2.2 is the accepted baseline for v2.3 planning.

## Accepted v2.2 Media state

v2.2 concentrated on the Media experience while preserving the self-hosted Hanafi bridge / Nougat-integrated Jellyfin architecture established in v2.1.

Accepted state includes:

- film-first Media library with poster-card and compact-list views;
- one library card per film, with editions/languages inside the film page;
- dedicated film pages using the single DK Media browser player;
- The Message (1976) with English and Urdu editions;
- Al-Risalah (1976) retained as the separately filmed Arabic production;
- Lion of the Desert (1981) and The Ten Commandments (1923) in the current library;
- artwork fetched from film-specific metadata and cached locally in IndexedDB rather than stored as a poster collection in GitHub;
- per-film crop/position support;
- distinct artwork mapping for Al-Risalah and The Message;
- Lion of the Desert framing adjustment for its source artwork;
- Wikimedia Commons resolution path for The Ten Commandments artwork;
- Nougat transport language with **^** as Play;
- centered **<< / ^ / >>** controls beneath the video in normal and fullscreen layouts;
- rewind and forward both set to 10 seconds;
- no separate Play/^ overlay over the video picture;
- clickable video surface for play/pause;
- mouse-wheel volume adjustment;
- pointer/fullscreen-control inactivity behavior;
- remembered resume position, volume and speed retained.
