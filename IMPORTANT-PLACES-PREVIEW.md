# Important Places of the Muslim World — Visual Preview

This is a **preview of candidate subjects**, not the final approved card list.

The expansion is intended for Muslim places that are historically, intellectually, culturally, politically, or architecturally important **without claiming that they have special religious sanctity**. Final cards will be researched and sourced individually before inclusion.
