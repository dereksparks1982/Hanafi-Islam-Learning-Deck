## Deliberate caution flags
- Salat al-Hajah: the widely circulated long hadith wording has a weak chain; the card says so.
- Salat al-Tasbih: recommended by many Hanafi jurists, but hadith grading is debated; the card says so.
- Istisqa: genuine difference within the Hanafi school; the card states both positions.
- Janazah for a child: classical supplication wording varies, so the card avoids pretending that one specific variant is uniquely required.
- Plane/vehicle prayer can depend heavily on circumstances. The card gives principles and directs case-specific questions to an imam.
