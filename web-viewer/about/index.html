Hanafi Learning Deck

About

Why the project exists, what it is trying to become, and the principles that govern it.

Project principle

No advertising. Islam is not for sale.

The Hanafi Learning Deck exists to make useful Islamic learning material easier to study, verify, carry, revisit, and share. Access is not divided into paid and unpaid religious tiers.

No account. No paywall. No advertising inserted by the Hanafi Learning Deck. No behavioral tracking by the project. Voluntary support may help keep the project running, but payment is never the price of admission to the dīn.

What this is

A growing Hanafi study system

The project began as a small personal set of cards for learning the basics of Islam. As the study became more serious, the project adopted a consistent Hanafi framework so a learner could build one coherent foundation rather than unknowingly mix rulings from different legal schools.

It has since grown beyond printable cards into an installable Web App with prayer tools, a Qur'an Reader, live religious resources, sacred-place exploration, media, giving resources, and other study surfaces. The repository remains the source of record.

Hanafi framework

Fiqh material is organized through the Hanafi school, with named sources and scholarly review treated as an ongoing responsibility.

Traceable sources

Where human translations, films, recordings, photographs, datasets, or other outside works are used, the project aims to preserve attribution and provenance rather than blur authorship.

Free access

Religious learning should remain available without a subscription, commercial lesson tier, or advertising wall around the material.

Still under review

The Hanafi Learning Deck is an educational study aid, not a fatwa service. Manual auditing and qualified imam or scholar review remain part of the project.

Service and support

A livelihood is not the same thing as selling the religion

The project distinguishes between supporting people who spend their working lives serving a Muslim community and turning religious knowledge itself into a commercial product. An imam, teacher, caller to prayer, or other community servant may need a livelihood. That is different from making access to basic religious knowledge depend on ads, paid tiers, or subscriptions.

The Hanafi Learning Deck therefore accepts the idea of voluntary project support while keeping the learning material itself free. Donations support the work; they do not purchase a better version of Islam.

Project direction

Cards are the foundation, not the boundary

The long-term design is to connect study surfaces instead of leaving them as unrelated tools:

Card → Qur'an → Place → Live → Media → Sources → Related learning

The printable card artwork remains important, while the digital Web App can connect a learner to deeper material, primary sources, places, media, and further study.

Corrections and review

The project expects correction

Islamic claims, Arabic, transliteration, source citations, historical material, and technical behavior should all be correctable. The goal is not to manufacture an appearance of authority. The goal is to keep improving the work and make its sources visible enough that errors can be found.
