Film information

The Message / الرسالة

The Message is a historical epic directed and produced by Moustapha Akkad about the beginnings of Islam. Akkad made separate English- and Arabic-language productions with different principal casts rather than simply dubbing one version into the other. Prophet Muhammad ﷺ is not directly depicted or voiced in the film.

English version The Message · about 2h 57m · Anthony Quinn, Irene Papas, Michael Ansara
Arabic version الرسالة / Al-Risalah · about 3h 27m · Abdallah Gheith, Mona Wassef · Arabic subtitles are visible in this copy
Director / producer Moustapha Akkad
Music Maurice Jarre · Academy Award nominee for Original Score

Study note

This is a historical drama and should be treated as a companion to study, not as Qur'an, hadith, a fatwa, or a substitute for a properly sourced study of the Seerah. Dramatic dialogue and presentation should not be treated as primary religious evidence.
