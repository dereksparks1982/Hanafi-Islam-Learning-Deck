Charity

A simple directory for Zakat, Sadaqah, Muslim relief, and general humanitarian giving. Organization cards can be added later one at a time after review.

Muslim giving

Zakat & Sadaqah

Zakat and general charity are not automatically interchangeable. Where an organization provides a dedicated Zakat option, that is noted below. Donors should use the organization’s own designation when intending Zakat.

  • Islamic Relief USA · Muslim humanitarian relief with dedicated Zakat and Sadaqah options.
  • Zakat Foundation of America · Zakat, Sadaqah, orphan care, education, and humanitarian programs.
  • Human Appeal USA · Dedicated scholar-reviewed Zakat program and humanitarian relief.
  • Penny Appeal USA · Zakat-eligible programs including food, water, orphan support, and emergency response.
  • MATW Project USA · Zakat, Sadaqah, water, orphan support, masjid projects, and emergency relief.
  • Helping Hand for Relief and Development · Muslim humanitarian organization with Zakat and multiple relief programs.
  • Palestine Children’s Relief Fund · Nonreligious humanitarian organization with a dedicated Zakat Fund for eligible relief.
  • UNRWA Gaza Emergency Appeal · Palestine refugee relief; the official donation form includes an option to designate a donation as Zakat.

General giving

Humanitarian charities

These organizations are listed for general charitable giving. Their presence here does not mean a donation should automatically be treated as Zakat.

  • World Central Kitchen · Emergency food relief for communities affected by disasters and crises.
  • St. Jude Children’s Research Hospital · Treatment and research for children with cancer and other serious diseases.
  • Direct Relief · Medical and emergency humanitarian assistance.
  • Mercy-USA for Aid and Development · Health, nutrition, clean water, education, and humanitarian development programs.
