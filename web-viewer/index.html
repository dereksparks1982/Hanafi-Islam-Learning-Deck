Put the Hanafi Learning Deck on your iPhone
  1. Open this Web App in Safari.
  2. Tap Safari’s ••• menu.
  3. Tap Share.
  4. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  5. Leave Open as Web App turned on.
  6. Tap Add.
The Hanafi Learning Deck icon will then appear on your Home Screen and open like an app. No App Store is required.

Choose the city used for prayer calculations

This uses the selected city's coordinates and time zone instead of treating your browser time zone as your physical location.

Need exact coordinates instead? Enter them manually

Choose a city, use device location, or enter exact coordinates to calculate prayer times on this device.

Prayer calculations use Adhan JS 4.4.6 by Batoul Apps under the MIT License. Calculation conventions differ by region, so the method can be changed to match a trusted local timetable.
