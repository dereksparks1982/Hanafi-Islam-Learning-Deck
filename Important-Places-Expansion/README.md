# Important Places of the Muslim World — v1.6.4

This expansion covers Muslim places that are historically, intellectually, culturally, politically, or architecturally important **without automatically claiming that they have special religious sanctity**.

## Current status

- **Card 1:** Lal Masjid — Islamabad, Pakistan — **APPROVED**
- **Card 2:** Chinguetti Mosque — Chinguetti, Mauritania — **APPROVED**
- **Card 3:** Abu Hanifa Mosque — Baghdad, Iraq — **APPROVED**
- Cards are developed and approved **one at a time**.

## Card approval rule

These photographic cards are too visually and historically detailed for unscreened batch publication.

For every new card:

1. research one place;
2. choose a strong, representative image of the actual subject;
3. build the candidate card in the approved Important Places visual style;
4. show the candidate to the maintainer before publication;
5. the maintainer screens the image, wording, sources, Arabic, border, typography, spacing, and overall appearance;
6. only an explicitly approved card is committed to this folder;
7. then the next card is started.

An approved supplied card image is authoritative. It is uploaded **as supplied**, with no substitute photograph or redesign unless explicitly requested.
